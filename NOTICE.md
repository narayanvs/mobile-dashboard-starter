# Artwork and component provenance

The scene, device animations and illustrative artwork were adapted from the
maintainer’s sanitised Home Assistant dashboard. Household entity identifiers,
network details, personal calendar data and backend automations are not included.

No third-party custom card implementation is bundled. Home Assistant supplies
native dialogs and icons at runtime. House and car artwork is illustrative;
brand-like shapes are not an endorsement by an equipment manufacturer.
